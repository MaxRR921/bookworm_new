# 01 · Reading ribbon

**Status:** keep (done)
**Flag:** removed (the ribbon is now the default)
**Impeccable command:** `delight`

## Idea
A silk bookmark ribbon marks the moment you're following. It is never decoration.

## Where to look (chapter discussion page)
- **Discussion bar:** pick a moment ("Show only this moment" in Group topics, or tap a faint moment tag). The selected moment unfurls in as a ribbon tab.
- **Locked replies:** "N more replies unlock at Chapter X" is marked with a tiny ribbon instead of a lock.
