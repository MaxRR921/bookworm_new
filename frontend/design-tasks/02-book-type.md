# 02 · Book type for reading text

**Status:** dead (done)
**Flag:** removed (code reverted)
**Impeccable command:** `typeset`

## Idea
Two type roles. The UI (names, labels, buttons, meta, pills) stays in Figtree. What people actually *read* (the chapter title, the recap, and post and reply text) is set in **Literata**, a typeface designed for long-form reading on screens (the Google Play Books e-reader face). The conversation reads like pages; the controls still read like an app.

## Where to look (chapter discussion page)
- **Chapter title and recap:** Literata, with a 32px title and a 16px recap at a 1.65 line height.
- **Posts:** Literata, 16px at a 1.6 line height.
- **Replies:** Literata, 15px.
- **Everything else** is unchanged Figtree.
