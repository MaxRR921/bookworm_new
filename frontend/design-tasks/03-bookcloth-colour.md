## Idea
A green clothbound hardback with its red ribbon. The cloth owns one region, the top bar, and its green replaces the generic blue for actions and selection. Neutrals lean faintly green (never cream), so the app stops reading as Reddit at a glance.

## Where to look
- **Top bar:** a deep cloth-green band with a light logo tile, the book title and a "Chapter N" chip.
- **Actions and selection:** "New post", the solid moment tags, "Group topics" when on, and "Show only this moment" are all cloth green.
- **The red ribbon** (selected moment, locked replies) stays red. Green cloth with a red ribbon is the classic hardback pairing.
- **Dark mode** has its own palette: a deep green-black ground with lighter cloth-green actions.
